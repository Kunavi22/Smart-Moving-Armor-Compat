# Smart Moving Armor Compatibility

Finally, you can enjoy **Minecraft 1.7.10** with **Smart Moving**, **Thaumcraft**, **Botania**, and other mods that add custom 3D armor, accessories, wings, backpacks, and player-model attachments.

This mod improves compatibility between Smart Moving's custom player renderer and equipment rendered by other mods.

## Requirements

This mod requires the [unofficial Smart Moving fork for Minecraft 1.7.10](https://github.com/makamys/SmartMoving) (recommended) or [SmartMoving](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/smart-moving), along with its dependencies:

* [Minecraft Forge](https://files.minecraftforge.net/net/minecraftforge/forge/index_1.7.10.html)
* [PlayerAPI](https://github.com/makamys/PlayerAPI)
* [SmartRender](https://github.com/makamys/SmartRender)
* [RenderPlayerAPI](https://github.com/makamys/RenderPlayerAPI)

## Compatible Mods

The following mods and equipment have been tested or are expected to work:

* **Thaumcraft**

  * Thaumostatic Harness
  * Most Thaumcraft addons, possibly all of them

* **Botania**

  * Armor
  * Wings
  * Baubles

* **Traveler's Gear**

* **Witching Gadgets**

  * Cloaks
  * Robes
  * Kamas
  * Other wearable equipment

* **Witchery**

  * Vampire Dress
  * Vampire Coat

* **Armored Arms**

* **Backhand**

* **BiblioCraft**

  * Armor Stand

* **Draconic Evolution**

  * Armor

* **ProjectRed Expansion**

  * Jetpack

* **Hardcore Ender Expansion**

  * Enderman Head

* **Galacticraft**

  * All armor sets

* **Adventure Backpack**

  * Backpacks
  * Jetpack
  * Copter
  * The Adventurer's Fedora still behaves incorrectly

* **Electro-Magic Tools**

  * Wings

* **Tinkers' Construct**

  * Equippable items

* **Et Futurum**

  * Elytra

* **GalaxySpace**

  * Electric Jetpack

## Known Issues

* The **Adventurer's Fedora** from Adventure Backpack may still render or behave incorrectly.
* Some untested custom equipment may have minor rendering problems.

## Reporting Compatibility Issues

When reporting an issue, please include:

* The affected mod and item
* The exact mod version
* Your Smart Moving and RenderPlayerAPI versions
* A screenshot of the rendering problem
* A crash report or game log, when applicable
